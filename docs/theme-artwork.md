# Theme artwork

Runtime artwork is bundled in `assets/images/theme/`. The uploaded UI references
remain in `assets/design_references/` and are not rendered as app screens.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using the uploaded SmartVax icon as
the character and visual style reference. Transparent alpha is preserved.

## Doctor and child prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent hero illustration for the SmartVax Flutter app. Input image is the reference for character identity and visual style. Create a clean standalone cutout of ONLY the same friendly brown-haired female pediatrician in white coat with blue scrubs and stethoscope vaccinating the same laughing toddler boy in yellow teddy shirt. Preserve their faces, hair, clothing, gentle joyful pose, the tiny blue syringe and the entire upper-body silhouettes. Rounded polished 3D storybook illustration with glossy soft highlights exactly matching the reference. Composition: both characters side by side, doctor on left, child on right; tight wide composition, no cropping of heads or arms. Genuinely transparent background. No glass frame, bubbles, shield, logo, SmartVax text, lettering, controls, shadows behind scene or background. This is runtime artwork placed inside actual widgets, not a UI screenshot.

## Family portrait prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one horizontal family portrait illustration for offline app avatars. Create a clean horizontal triptych-like group portrait of three smiling fictional children in exactly the glossy 3D storybook style of the attached SmartVax illustration. Three equal sized upper-body head portraits spaced with ample transparent space between them, ordered left to right: Aarav, cheerful 20-month-old brown-haired boy in blue shirt with big brown eyes; Diya, smiling 17-month-old brown-haired girl with two little ponytails, pink bows and pink shirt; Vihaan, smiling 2-month baby boy with a little brown hair curl, teal shirt. All face forward. Each child's full head and shoulders must fit within their own third of the image. Smooth polished soft highlights, warm faces, cyan pink pastel accent light. Transparent background. No circles, no frames, no logos, no text, no UI, no accessories overlapping between characters. Width-to-height ratio 3:1. This single family portrait will be reused consistently in child avatar widgets.

## Liquid background prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait background artwork behind REAL interactive Flutter widgets. Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE only. Create a standalone 9:16 decorative liquid glass background matching the same beautiful glossy cyan rainbow style as the supplied SmartVax Child profile reference. Rich luminous sky blue and aqua cyan, pastel lavender pink, soft mint and tiny warm yellow lights. Very polished 3D transparent soap bubbles and rounded glass globules clustered along far left and right edges, translucent flowing liquid glass ribbons along the very bottom, scattered tiny glints. A single translucent blue shield with a glowing white heart near upper right, glass bubble with a white plus near upper left. Center must remain a broad luminous PALE CYAN open area with gentle gradients for readable app cards. Decorative density concentrated in peripheral edges, no large objects in central 65% width. No text of any kind, no letters, no logo, no people, no portraits, no cards, no buttons, no top status bar, no bottom navigation, no UI, no spinner. This is only seamless-feeling background artwork, not a screenshot. Preserve the uploaded reference's iridescent photographic glass materials and cheerful high quality 3D finish.
